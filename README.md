# Who Delivers When It Matters

Interactive dashboard for **SupeRR Selector Season 2 – Round 1**, prepared for Rajasthan Royals.

**Live dashboard:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/superr-dashboard/

## What it does
- Defines pressure from the match itself: a win-probability model scores every ball, and the 25% of balls where one delivery can swing the result most are treated as high pressure.
- Rates every batter and bowler on those balls against an average player in the same situation and against his own normal level.
- Ranks the top 10 batters and top 10 bowlers, stress-tested with 200 bootstrap re-runs.

## Data
842 completed IPL and T20I matches, 2022–2026 (199,029 deliveries).
